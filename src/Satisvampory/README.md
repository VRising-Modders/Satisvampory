Castle logistics for V Rising. Your chests, stations and castle hearts work together, so you spend less time running between them.

Satisvampory needs both parts: install it on the server and on every player's client. It runs on Progenitor.

## What it does

- **Craft and build from storage.** Workstations, the Character window's Crafting tab, building, repairs and castle heart upgrades take materials from any chest on your island. Reserves are kept, and the last stack in a chest can be left alone.
- **Input and Output.** Every chest, station and castle heart has an Input and an Output button in its own window. Each opens an ordered list of chests and belts it pulls from or sends to. Stations pull only whole crafts.
- **Mass Deposit.** Each chest's deposit settings say what belongs in it and at what priority. Quick stash, tidy and servant loot all follow them.
- **Quick stash.** One key, or a double-tap on the game's sort button, puts everything except your hotbar where it belongs.
- **Inventory and Find.** See everything on your island with reserves and caps, find any item, and take it into your bags.
- **Belts and categories.** Name groups of chests and groups of items, and use them anywhere a list or filter is asked for.
- **Castle and clan share.** Your own castles act as one, and so can your clan's.
- **Castle hearts.** Hearts top up with blood essence from storage when you visit.
- **Servants.** Hunt loot is stashed, and hunts can repeat on their own if the server allows it.

## Settings

Everything is on this page: your own settings, keys (unbound until you pick one) and a help section. Admins change the server settings here too. There are no chat commands.

## Coming from Satisvampory 1.x or KindredLogistics

An admin can import chest names and the older mod's settings once, from the Server section. Never run either of those mods alongside this one.
